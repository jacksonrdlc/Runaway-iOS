## 1. **Freemium + AI-First Premium Model** 
**Priority: HIGH | Implementation: 2-3 weeks**

**Strategy**: Position AI coaching as the premium differentiator with a generous free tier for basic tracking.

**Free Tier**:
- Basic GPS tracking and route mapping
- Manual run logging
- Simple weekly/monthly stats
- Limited daily commitment tracking (7-day streaks)

**Premium Features ($4.99/month or $39.99/year)**:
- Unlimited AI coaching conversations with Claude
- Advanced training insights and recovery recommendations
- Unlimited commitment streak tracking
- Detailed performance analytics and trends
- Custom training plan generation
- Advanced awards and achievement system
- Priority customer support

**Rationale**: AI coaching provides clear, ongoing value that justifies recurring payments. The free tier builds habit formation before conversion.

## 5. **Value-Based Pricing with Usage Analytics**

**Premium Feature Validation**:

- **Conversion tracking**: Identify the premium features that drive subscription upgrades
- **Churn analysis**: Understand which features prevent cancellations

**Revenue Optimization**:
- **Annual discount**: 40% off yearly subscriptions (vs. monthly)
- **Win-back campaigns**: Offer 50% discount to churned users after 30 days
- **Upgrade prompts**: Context-aware premium feature suggestions during peak engagement
- **Family plans**: Shared subscriptions for running families/groups at $9.99/month

**Key Metrics to Track**:
- Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) growth rate
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) vs Lifetime Value (LTV)
- Premium conversion rate by user segment
- Feature usage correlation with retention
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) among premium users
