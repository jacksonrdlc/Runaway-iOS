## Monetization & Growth

# Runaway iOS Growth Strategy: 5 Actionable Recommendations

## 1. Tiered Subscription Model with AI-First Premium Value
**Priority: High | Implementation: 6-8 weeks**

### Recommended Structure
- **Free Tier**: Basic run tracking, 7-day data history, limited AI insights (3 questions/week)
- **Runner ($4.99/month)**: Unlimited AI coaching, full data history, basic awards, Strava sync
- **Athlete ($9.99/month)**: Advanced analytics, custom training plans, readiness scoring, priority support
- **Annual Discounts**: 20% off (industry standard for fitness apps)

### Premium Features Worth Premium Pricing
- **AI Training Plans**: Personalized weekly/monthly programs based on goals and performance
- **Advanced Recovery Insights**: Daily readiness scores using HRV, sleep, and subjective metrics
- **Injury Prevention Alerts**: AI analysis of pace/stride patterns to flag overtraining
- **Race Preparation Programs**: Structured plans for 5K to marathon distances
- **Detailed Performance Analytics**: Trend analysis, seasonal comparisons, PR predictions

### Revenue Optimization
- Implement paywall after 3-5 AI coaching sessions to demonstrate value
- Use progressive disclosure - show advanced charts/insights but require upgrade to access
- Target conversion during post-run "runner's high" moments with contextual upgrade prompts
