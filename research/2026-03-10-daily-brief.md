**Priority: High | Implementation: Medium | Revenue Impact: High**

### Premium Tier ($9.99/month or $79.99/year)

- Advanced analytics and trend analysis
- Custom training plan generation
- Priority support

### Implementation Approach
- Use StoreKit 2 for subscription management
- Implement server-side receipt validation through Supabase Edge Functions
- Create clear value demonstration in onboarding
- Offer 7-14 day free trial to showcase premium value
